# ANTLR 4.13.2.20

**21 November 2026**

ANTLR **4.13.2.20** is a major performance, code-generation, and architectural release of the optimized ANTLR 4 fork, following **4.13.2.19** (`84f5b5fea`).

* **Maven coordinates:** `me.sunlan:antlr4:4.13.2.20`, `me.sunlan:antlr4-runtime:4.13.2.20`, `me.sunlan:antlr4-maven-plugin:4.13.2.20`, and `me.sunlan:antlr4-annotations:4.13.2.20`
* **Target platform:** Java 8+
* **Self-contained runtime:** Internal HPPC collections (`com.carrotsearch:hppc:0.9.1`) remain strictly encapsulated, minimized, and shaded into `org.antlr.v4.runtime.shaded.com.carrotsearch.hppc` with zero transitive HPPC dependency and zero exposure on any `public` or `protected` API.

---

## Highlights

* **2.257× (+125.7%) cumulative geometric-mean speedup over 4.13.2.19** across all 11 serial and parallel real-world Apache Groovy lexing and parsing benchmarks—delivered monotonically across two waves (`1.904×` in `7dcb914c0`, followed by an additional `1.186×` mean / `1.189×` median in `e900884fe`, winning 11/11 scenarios on both mean and median with zero regressions).
* **Dramatically leaner generated Java recognizers:** On the Apache Groovy grammar, `GroovyParser` total method bytecode dropped by **33.8%** (`65,081 B → 43,098 B`), oversized methods above HotSpot C2's `FreqInlineSize` (`> 325 B`) dropped by **56.3%** (`48 → 21`), adaptive prediction sites fell by **43.6%** (`326 → 184` fused `_syncPredict` sites), and `GroovyLexer` method count fell by **41.7%** (`84 → 49`).
* **Architectural unification and concurrency hardening:** Eliminated duplicated hot-path forks across the runtime and tool, brought `Parser` (966 lines) and `LexerATNSimulator` (999 lines) back under 1,000 lines, hardened Java Memory Model (JMM) publication on concurrent DFA/lexer caches, and maintained 100% parse equivalence.

## Runtime Improvements (`runtime/Java`)

### Lexing and Character Streams

* **Direct `CodePointLexerRunner`:** Extracted package-private `CodePointLexerRunner` in `org.antlr.v4.runtime` with register-resident 8-bit (`byte[]`), 16-bit (`char[]`), and 32-bit (`int[]`) DFA scan loops and shared in-place `-> skip` fusion. `CodePoint{8,16,32}BitCharStream` and their backing arrays stay strictly package-private (zero mutable internal array exposure).
* **Safe subclass gating:** Added `LexerATNSimulator.fastDfaEligibleClass()` paired with metadata-driven `ClassValue` override detection (`NO_HOOK_OVERRIDES`) and single-entry lock-free caches (`LexerATNSimulator.lastFastEligibleClass`, `Lexer.lastDefaultSkipClass`) so custom hook overrides never silently bypass subclass behavior while helper-only subclasses (such as Groovy's `PositionAdjustingLexerATNSimulator`) keep full fast-path speed.
* **Lock-free predicate-edge cache (`LexerPredEdgeTable`):** Caches deterministic `(ruleIndex, predIndex)` decision trees per `(stateNumber, asciiChar)` in copy-on-write `AtomicReferenceArray<PredNode>[]` rows bound to the mode's `dfaS0` identity, eliminating repeated ATN reach/closure on predicated ASCII tokens (such as `/` and `'` in Groovy) with zero `PredTrace` allocation on warm single-predicate hits.
* **Compiled `LexerActionExecutor` dispatch:** Precomputes `hasUnfixedPositionDependent`, `isSingleSkip()`, and fast execution modes (`MODE_SINGLE`, `MODE_MULTI`, `MODE_CUSTOM_OPT_BUILTIN`, `MODE_SLOW`) with a single `lexer.action(...)` call site to avoid HotSpot C2 `NodeCountInliningCutoff` jitter.
* **Faster `CodePointCharStream` and `CharStreams`:** Direct `fromString` construction without intermediate `ByteBuffer` wrappers, 64-bit overflow-safe `getText(int, int)` bounds calculation, and complete handling of mid-match `Lexer._input` swaps (including `more()`).

### Token Streams

* **Chunked `TokenList`:** Replaced `BufferedTokenStream`'s default `ArrayList<Token>` with package-private `TokenList` (fixed 256-slot chunks, 1 KiB per chunk on 64-bit CompressedOops), eliminating resize copy stalls and G1GC remembered-set copy barriers while releasing excess chunks on `clear()`.
* **Fused `CommonTokenStream` and cursorless lookahead:** Fused `consume()` and `LT(k > 1)` directly on `TokenList`, added cursor-safe `CommonTokenStream.nextOnChannel(int)` and `cachedLTMinus1`, sealed `tokens == fastTokens` reads behind `tokenCount()` / `tokenAt(int)`, and hardened `BufferedTokenStream.sync(int)` against 32-bit overflow.
* **HotSpot C2 MDO profile isolation:** Kept `LA(1)` / `LT(1)` hit paths strictly `<= 35 B` (`MaxInlineSize`) and split rare `k == 1` cache misses (`ltOneSlow` / `laOneSlow`) from `k != 1` lookahead (`ltSlow` / `ltCommonSlow` / `laSlow`), preventing cold slow-path IR from polluting generated parser methods.

### Parser ATN / DFA Simulation and Caching

* **Cursorless warm SLL fast path (`execDFASllWarm`):** Handles both single-token and multi-token warm SLL accepts (via `CommonTokenStream.nextOnChannel(int)`) with zero `SimulatorState` allocation, zero `mark`/`consume`/`seek`/`release`, zero `try/finally`, and zero `this.dfa` G1 write barriers, while outlining `execDFASllWarmSlow`, `adaptivePredictSlow`, and `completeAccept`.
* **Unified `EpsilonClosure` and zero-allocation merge-on-hit:** Consolidated base direct closure and overriding-subclass virtual dispatch into a single lazy-state tail-looping walker (`walk`/`walkVirtual`), paired with `ATNConfigSet.addTransformed` (probing `(targetState, alt)` before allocating a transformed `ATNConfig`).
* **3-pass O(n) zero-sort `ConflictDetector`:** Replaced sorting-based conflict detection (removing 492 lines of duplicated code and fixing a latent last-state `exact` bug) with a per-simulator open-addressed O(n) detector, completely eliminating `DualPivotQuicksort.sort` during DFA construction.
* **Monomorphic `PredictionContextCache` and fast context interning:** Replaced `HashMap` + wrapper keys with three open-addressed tables (`ContextInternSet`, `ChildContextMap`, `JoinContextMap`) factored over `private` `OpenTableBase` (0.5 load factor, O(size) sparse clear, oversized-table shrinking); added iterative zero-map `SingletonPredictionContext` canonicalization in `PredictionContext.getCachedContextInternal`.
* **Compact ATN/DFA metadata and O(1) lookups:** Packed `ATNConfig.passedThroughNonGreedyDecision` into base alignment padding (keeping 32-byte CompressedOops size) with inline MurmurHash3, cached-hash fast-reject in `equals()`, and hash invalidation on `mergeConfigContext` outer-context-reach updates; added `DFAState.getUnconflictedPrediction()`, O(D) bottom-up `computeStartState`, `ATNState.singleNonEpsilonTransition` / `noNonEpsilonOptimizedTransitions`, `ATN.precedenceLL1` dense precedence tables, `LL1Analyzer.PredicatePolicy`, and O(1) `IntervalSet.fastBits` bitsets for read-only sets in `[-2..511]`.
* **Parser decomposition and concurrency hardening:** Extracted cold diagnostics into package-private `ParserDiagnostics`; moved the canonical generated-parser hooks onto `Parser` (with all 11 single-step hooks strictly `<= 35 B`); converted `DFAState.asciiEdges` and `contextSymbols` to copy-on-write `volatile` publication for full cross-architecture JMM safety; and replaced `synchronized (this)` in `Recognizer` error listeners with lock-free CAS copy-on-write arrays.

## Tool Analysis and Java Code Generation (`tool`)

* **ALL(\*)-sound EBNF exit LL(1) recovery (`AnalysisPipeline`):** Promotes greedy single-alternative EBNF loops/optionals (`X*`, `X+`, `X?`) followed by left-recursive or context-predicated sub-rules from adaptive prediction to static LL(1) via `LL1Analyzer.LOOK(..., PredicatePolicy.SEE_THROUGH_DROPPED_CALLEE_PREDS)`, seeing through only callee precedence and context-dependent predicates dropped by ALL(\*) at `callDepth > 0` while keeping hoisted context-independent predicates strictly opaque.
* **Inherited `Parser` hooks and `_syncPredict` fusion:** Removed 9 duplicated helper methods from `Java.stg` (`1,356 → 1,194` lines) in favor of canonical `protected final` hooks on `Parser`, fusing `setState` + `_sync` + `_adaptivePredict` into a single 22-byte `_syncPredict(state, decision)` call.
* **Compact loops and LL(1) bitmask tests:** Added `Target`-gated compact single-token star loops (`_matchStar` / `_matchStarPredict`) and compact 1–3 alternative 64-bit bitmask lookahead tests (`LL1AltBlock`), substantially shrinking rule bytecode and expanding C2 inlining eligibility.
* **Tier 2a / 2b labeled-alternative context optimization:** Eliminates redundant base `RuleContext` allocation and `copyFrom()` via direct `AltContext` construction (Tier 2a) and deferred entry via `_enterDeferred` (Tier 2b) under sound `baseContext` / `LL1AltBlock` / listener guards.
* **Flat O(1) `action()` and `sempred()` dispatch:** Replaces two-level `ruleIndex` dispatch with a flat `tableswitch` on `actionIndex` / `predIndex`, backed by exhaustive 31-subclass `ActionChunk` analysis in `OutputModelController` (also fixing a latent bug where `SetAttr`/`SetNonLocalAttr` `rhsChunks` were not inspected for `predIndex`) and a zero-allocation, JLS §3.3-compliant `JavaIdentifierScanner`.
* **Multi-target safety:** `Target.supportsCompactSingleTokenStarLoop()`, `Target.supportsCompactLL1AltTests()`, and `Target.supportsFlatActionAndSempredDispatch()` default to `false` and are enabled only in `JavaTarget`, preserving compatibility with pluggable third-party targets.

## Compatibility and Upgrade Notes

* **Existing generated recognizers:** Parsers and lexers compiled with earlier `4.13.2.x` releases remain source- and binary-compatible with `me.sunlan:antlr4-runtime:4.13.2.20` and immediately inherit the runtime speedups.
* **Regenerating grammars:** Regenerating grammars with `me.sunlan:antlr4:4.13.2.20` is recommended to obtain the full Java code-generation and bytecode reductions. Note that parsers regenerated with `4.13.2.20` rely on the new `protected final` hooks on `Parser` (annotated `@since 4.13.2.20`) and therefore require `antlr4-runtime:4.13.2.20` or newer at runtime.
* **Reserved `Parser` hooks:** The 13 `protected final` hooks on `Parser` (`_match`, `_matchEOF`, `_matchSet`, `_matchSetEOF`, `_adaptivePredict`, `_sync()`, `_sync(int)`, `_syncPredict`, `_prec`, `_recover`, `_matchStar`, `_matchStarPredict`, `_enterDeferred`) are reserved for generated Java parsers and cannot be overridden by subclasses.
